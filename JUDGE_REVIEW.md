## Small learning check

Ask before the mission and again after the debrief:

1. “Why can't every system stay on at once?”
2. “What information would make you change the crew's plan?”

Look for explanations involving shared power, competing goals, and changed conditions. Record how much help the learner needed. A small test supports a narrow observation about those participants; it does not establish broad educational effectiveness.
