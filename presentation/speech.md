# Nimbus Nova: pitch speech (English)

English translation of the team's Romanian speech, split by slide. The same text is in each slide's speaker notes in `NimbusNova-Pitch.pptx`.

Translation notes:
- The original line "the first Minecraft mod that trains students on space missions" is rendered as "a Minecraft mod that trains students on space missions", because we could not back up the "first" claim.
- The "Mining for Words" study compared children who learned English words incidentally by playing Minecraft with children who studied the same words intentionally. The original speech says "worksheet"; the wording below follows what the paper reports.
- The original line "kids will learn without even realising it" now says that kids learn by playing and then explaining the mission in the debrief, because the debrief and teacher pauses are how the game teaches.
- Lines marked *(optional)* are not in the original speech. They name the NASA data on slide 5 and can be skipped.

## Slide 1: Title

Every kid wants to play, but not every kid wants to learn.

## Slide 2: The problem

After a while this becomes a real problem. Kids stop opening educational platforms and just play games instead.

So how did we solve it?

## Slide 3: The solution

We built Nova, a Minecraft mod that trains students on space missions.

You might say we just built a simple space-themed game. That's fine, because kids learn by playing the mission and then explaining what happened in the debrief.

## Slide 4: How it works

So what exactly is Nova? Nova is a system where teachers create sessions for their class. Students join using our modpack and train on plausible scenarios, learning how to handle them by working as a team.

Research on Minecraft points the same way. In the "Mining for Words" study (2025), children who picked up English words by playing Minecraft improved more than children who studied the same words directly. That study is about language, so testing Nova with a real class is our next step.

## Slide 5: Real NASA data

Thanks to the work of NASA's teams, we were able to create realistic missions for the future, built around real problems that astronauts and rovers have faced, or problems that are very plausible.

*(optional)* For example, the Mars mission's solar power follows the energy readings from the Opportunity rover during the 2007 and 2018 dust storms, and the radiation shelter rule comes from Curiosity's measurements of a 2017 solar storm.

## Slide 6: Similar apps

There are similar apps out there.

Crater Crew is a multiplayer educational game about Mars that is also aimed at kids and schools. It is more of a strategy and exploration game than a simulator where a crew has to repair a spacecraft.

Artemis: Return to the Moon is a Minecraft Education project made in partnership with NASA. Kids work in teams, solve challenges and use programming to navigate the Orion capsule.

The main difference is that we focus on the missions themselves. Artemis is essentially a coding exercise on a space theme.

## Slide 7: What's next

In the future we can grow the project by adding more missions. We also have the idea of bringing this modpack into the base game, the way Minecraft Education works, so it becomes much more accessible.

Thank you!
