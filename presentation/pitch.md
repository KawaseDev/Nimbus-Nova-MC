# Nimbus Nova — presentation draft

A short pitch for a live demo. Copy each numbered section into a slide. The screenshots are in this folder.

## 1. Nimbus Nova

**A space mission trainer for the classroom, built in Minecraft.**

Visual: `moon-surface.png`

Say: “Students become a crew. They use evidence, share limited resources and make decisions together.”

## 2. The classroom problem

- Spaceflight involves systems that affect each other.
- Students need a way to explore those relationships together.
- Teachers need control over the pace of the lesson.

Say: “Reading about oxygen, power and navigation is different from having to balance them as a team.”

## 3. How it works

- The teacher selects a mission and manages the session.
- Students join, choose roles and operate different stations.
- Shared resources and mission events turn decisions into consequences.
- The teacher can pause for discussion and review the crew’s decisions.

Say: “The teacher runs the lesson; the crew has to communicate because each role has a different job.”

## 4. Moon expedition

**Prepare → dock → navigate → land → collect a sample → return**

Visual: `moon-descent.png`

Say: “The pilot checks guidance and landing speeds. Maintenance verifies communications. Life support checks suits before the crew goes outside.”

## 5. Science and decisions

- Compare the simulated sample with a NASA lunar basalt reference.
- Choose whether to continue the landing or cancel and return.
- Compare Earth-entry predictions and protect power for the return.

Visual: `return-burn.png`

Say: “The instruments and resource values are teaching models. We link to NASA sources for the science and history.”

## 6. Live demo

1. Open `/nimbus` or the teacher panel and select **Moon expedition**.
2. Show a role and one spacecraft station.
3. Show a landing decision or the Moon surface and sample.
4. Show the teacher’s pause and debrief if time allows.

Say: “This is a playable prototype. The next step is testing it with teachers and a full student crew.”
