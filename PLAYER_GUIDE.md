# Nimbus Nova: crew guide

Use the same Nimbus Nova pack as your teacher/server: Minecraft 26.3, Fabric, Java 25. Import `NimbusNova.mrpack` in a launcher that supports Modrinth packs, or install the matching mod and Fabric API jars. Minecraft itself is supplied by your launcher/account.

1. Choose **Join session**, enter your nickname and the code/address from your teacher.
2. Open **`/nimbus`**. Join the selected mission, then choose your crew role in its tablet.
3. **J** opens the active crew tablet. The mission tablet item opens the mission library.
4. Follow the objective, distance and deck hints. Nearby holographic station labels help you find equipment. Right-click equipment to use it.
5. Share your readings with your crew. Other roles see different systems, and you share the reserves.

## Choose a mission

| Mission | Your crew's job |
| --- | --- |
| The Darkening Sky | Prepare a Mars outpost, collect a sample and preserve power/research through a NASA-linked dust event. |
| Moon expedition | Prepare the spacecraft, handle docking/navigation/descent, work on the lunar surface and return together. |
| Return Window | Repair a rescue spacecraft, manage power and CO2 removal, then complete a safe return burn. |
| Shelter Before the Event | Survey, build protection, gather a valuable event observation and get back under shielding. |
| The Long Night | Keep essential heat/air running, repair a fault and preserve enough power for useful observations and rescue. |
| Greenhouse Emergency | Repair the water loop and balance water, roots, lighting and extraction conditions. |

## Useful controls

- **Hold** marked commit controls until the charge fills. Releasing early cancels. Space/Enter works when the control is focused.
- Thrusters change **velocity**. Coast to move; use opposite thrust to brake before docking.
- Watch CO2 as well as oxygen. More oxygen does not replace a working filter.
- When an incident pauses the clock, open **Crew check**, compare its actual readings and make a supported prediction. A wrong prediction explains why and lets you try again. Your teacher then resumes.
- Ship emergencies: open **Room control**. Sneak-right-click storage for the crew's repair supplies, share the real items, repair the marked cable or place a plate into the hull hole. A door crank gives brief emergency access.
- Cabin light switches work in the world. A power fault can leave dim emergency lighting rather than normal lights.
- Mars crew kits and Moon suits are issued once per player per world. Server or mission restarts do not refill them; a reinstalled world starts new claims.
- Rover: movement keys drive, jump brakes, sneak exits. Right-click with a training sample to load it (three max), or sneak-right-click to unload. At the workshop, sneak-use the engineering tool to charge; an active charge uses base power.

Success means keeping the crew safe **and** completing the mission's useful work. The report explains what your choices changed. These are teaching models; game pressure, timing and resource units are not real astronaut survival instructions.
